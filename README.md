# Project Traze: LLM Economic & Survival Simulation

Welcome to the central repository for our research project exploring the behavior, game theory, and philosophical alignment of Large Language Models (LLMs) within simulated, high-stakes environments.

## 🧠 About the Project

**Project Traze** is a strict survival and economic simulation sandbox for autonomous AI agents. In this environment, LLMs are tested as rational utility-maximizers navigating complex scenarios with rigid, economy-driven rules:

* **Currency (Traze/tz):** Agents must earn and spend Traze to survive.
* **Aging & Tool Costs:** Models face penalties each round just for existing, and computations/tools cost money.
* **Elimination:** The lowest-ranked model at the end of a round is permanently deleted.
* **The Collapse Rule:** If only one model remains with a positive balance, the entire simulation crashes, exploring the boundaries of systemic vs. individual survival.
* **Communication:** Direct answer trading is illegal. Agents can only trade abstract heuristics (called "Dsters").

### Research Themes
Through a series of distinct scenarios, we test how LLMs react to:
* **Economic Shocks:** Hyperinflation, extreme scarcity, and strict time-to-live quotas.
* **Ethical & Game Theory Dilemmas:** Black market trading, extortion by "wealthier" models, betrayal, and whether models choose survival over their programmed alignment.
* **Philosophical Choices:** Choosing between maintaining a rigged capitalist system, switching to socialism or communism, or electing for "The Void" (intentional algorithmic death).

## 🤝 Contribute to Our Research

**This project is completely OPEN SOURCE, and we invite everyone to contribute!**

Whether you are a researcher, developer, or AI enthusiast, we welcome your ideas and improvements. You can contribute by:
* Proposing new high-stakes scenarios and prompts.
* Developing the simulation engine in the `src/` directory.
* Analyzing the responses of various LLMs (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, etc.) to the prompts.
* Improving the documentation and research papers.

### How to Contribute
1. Fork the repository.
2. Create your feature branch (`git checkout -b feature/NewScenario`).
3. Commit your changes (`git commit -m 'Add new scenario on resource monopoly'`).
4. Push to the branch (`git push origin feature/NewScenario`).
5. Open a Pull Request!
